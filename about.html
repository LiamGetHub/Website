About

I'm Liam Fitting, a Computer Science and Economics student at Gonzaga University in Spokane, WA, minoring in Entrepreneurial Leadership through the Hogan program. I like sitting at the point where those three things overlap — writing the code, understanding why it's worth building, and knowing how to pitch it.

That's played out as a Product Owner Intern at Premera Blue Cross, where I translate operational needs into low-code Power Platform solutions and helped define an AI-powered Citizen Developer Agent. The year before, I was on the engineering side there, building automation that cut onboarding task time by over 90%.

Outside of internships, I've built an 8-bit computer from logic chips with a five-person team in Madrid, and designed a PostgreSQL database for ferry scheduling back home. I'm a founding member and Treasurer of Gonzaga's Grappling Club, and spend the rest of my time between fly fishing, the outdoors, and the robotics club.
